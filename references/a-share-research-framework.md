# A-share Research Framework

## Target Identification

Confirm:

- Stock code and short name.
- Exchange and listing board.
- Industry classification.
- Main business and revenue drivers.
- Whether the company is under ST, suspended, newly listed, restructuring, or subject to unusual risk warnings.

If a short name maps to multiple companies, clarify before researching unless the user also provided a code.

## Research Sequence

Use the default opportunity focus and research horizon in `SKILL.md`, unless the user specifies otherwise. Organize the conclusion around four questions: what operating improvement is supported, how much the current price already assumes, what happens if the thesis fails, and why participating now or waiting is justified. Undervaluation recovery still requires assessing value deterioration; an event or rising chart alone does not establish the default investment case.

1. Company identity and business model.
2. Latest disclosures and financial reporting period.
3. Revenue, profit, margin, cash flow, balance sheet, and shareholder changes.
4. Industry cycle, policy environment, demand, supply, and competitive position.
5. Valuation against history and peers.
6. Market behavior: trend, volume, turnover, support, resistance, and sector strength.
   For a deep dive, read the separate market and stock sentiment snapshots. Show the latest completed A-share trade day and available points for the past five trade days; identify missing days and source scope before interpreting heat or breadth.
   Apply `sentiment-framework.md` to actively research company/sector and relevant overseas messages, prior expectations, competing narratives and behavioral hypotheses. Save the review attachment; compare time-matched stock/sector feedback and give a separate short-term horizon, sentiment confidence and invalidation rather than treating heat ranks as investor direction.
7. Catalysts: earnings, policy, orders, capacity, price changes, restructuring, dividends, buybacks, or product cycles.
8. Risks: financial quality, governance, policy, competition, valuation, liquidity, pledges, lawsuits, regulatory issues, and market regime.
9. Cross-market references when they explain the A-share target.
   Select overseas peers, tech indices, volatility, rates, policy probabilities, credit spreads, commodities or FX only with a stated business/valuation transmission path, countercase and availability time. Compare each market on its own completed sessions and avoid later data in earlier A-share conclusions.
10. Rating, position-building plan, and tracking checklist.

For a deep report, put the decisive figures in the evidence table before interpreting them. Where relevant, show more than one reporting period for revenue, profit, operating cash flow, margins, and balance-sheet risks; show the valuation metric's earnings/book-value basis, an appropriate historical or peer comparison, and a dated price/volume snapshot. Mark unavailable or inapplicable items rather than filling gaps with estimates.

Freeze the exact sentiment snapshot versions, narrative-review attachment and their gaps in the common evidence brief before independent bull, bear and risk review. Social posts and news are leads, not proof of company facts. Do not let sentiment alone determine a rating or relax the verified-filing, share-basis and timestamped-quote gate.

## Financial Quality And Manual Review

For decisive forecasts, explain separate revenue, sustainable parent-margin and multiple chains under [research-workpaper.md](research-workpaper.md#假设计算及业务差异). Each needs an adopted historical base, causal drivers/range, counterevidence and the first observable checkpoint. Where relevant, attribute returns using comparable average balances and distinguish leverage from operating efficiency; PB and sector-inappropriate industrial formulas follow [valuation-framework.md](valuation-framework.md). Use reverse requirements and joint sensitivity for the dispute that can change judgment, without creating new ratings or bands.

Use [research-workpaper.md](research-workpaper.md#按需财务核查与采用) for the adopted inputs, optional payment/entity and original-disclosure review tables, and the single calculation record. The additional Sina fields in [data-source-policy.md](data-source-policy.md#financial-statement-candidates) remain unverified leads until reconciled with filings; missing fields stay missing.

- Explain cash conversion, receivables/inventory changes and non-core income from adopted workpaper IDs over comparable periods. Separate cumulative income/cash flow from period-end balances; derive a quarter only from comparable same-year cumulative inputs. Preserve negative profit/income. Do not force ratios with non-positive denominators or mismatched scopes, including consolidated CFO versus attributable profit.
- For material payments, identify the payer, dated cash and restrictions, future uses/windows, committed drawable financing, operating-cash assumptions and unresolved coverage. Group cash does not establish entity-level availability; a credit line is not cash. Reconcile principal, finance costs, proceeds, dividends, buybacks and already-booked effects once under the workpaper rules.
- Manually verify material non-recurring items, minority interests/consolidation and share events with original-page adoption records. Keep tax, attribution, effective dates and potential dilution explicit; these are not new automatic data routes.
- Label CFO less cash paid for long-term asset purchases as a simplified measure, never complete FCFF; net investing cash flow cannot substitute for that payment item. Receivable growth or a modeled funding gap calls for explanation and evidence, not an automatic fraud/default finding or a composite score.

## Multi-perspective Judgment

Use four views before the final conclusion. For a single-stock deep dive, or when the user explicitly requests independent-agent debate, read `multi-agent-debate.md` and run its independent bull/bear and three-way risk workflow when runtime collaboration tools are available. For an ordinary short answer, keep these as concise analytical views:

- Bull Case: strongest upside logic supported by facts.
- Bear Case: strongest downside or thesis-failure logic supported by facts.
- Risk Manager: where the analysis is most likely wrong, what evidence would invalidate it, and what would make position sizing unsafe.
- Portfolio View: what kind of capital and holding period fit the setup.

The final conclusion should say which side has stronger evidence and why. Separate verified facts from modeled futures; a supported forecast can be evaluated without waiting for all indicators or the next formal report. Apply `valuation-framework.md` to material assumption sensitivity and `position-building-and-risk.md` to rating, confidence, new-money status and genuine blockers. Name the actual participating agents in a deep report; never imply independent agents ran when collaboration tools were unavailable or a role failed.

## Output Discipline

Lead with the conclusion. Put key data timestamps near the top. Keep source links near the facts they support. Avoid vague language such as "may benefit" unless the mechanism is stated.

For a quick question, browse current sources and answer the requested scope. Announcement/fact explanations, focused sentiment and chip-only supplements need the relevant conclusion, evidence, uncertainty and source links; they do not require a rating or position-building stance. Preserve the required single-stock chip section, save-on-request and standing overview rules in SKILL.md.

For an explicit investment judgment, or any rating actually given, also provide the rating (or specific unrated reason), confidence/reason, new-money stance, decisive reasons/risks and the full original-source, share, quote, valuation and price-band contract. Brevity does not relax those gates. Do not turn an unrequested rating into a reason to expand a factual question into a full report.

If the user asks for a deep report, use the full report template.
