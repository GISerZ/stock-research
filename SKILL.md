# stock-research

## Core Behavior

Use this skill for A-share stock research. Treat A-share listed companies as the primary research universe. Use overseas stocks, Hong Kong stocks, Korean stocks, global indices, ETFs, commodities, rates, currencies, and global risk signals only as reference variables unless the user explicitly makes them the main target.

Default to earnings improvement at a reasonable price, with undervaluation recovery as a secondary opportunity. When none is specified, research the next 12 calendar months from the valuation date, recording the actual start and end dates. Prioritize end-of-horizon value and price upside/downside relative to the timestamped reference quote, supported by operating milestones during the year. Label the default as a research assumption, not the user's funding horizon or a requirement to hold for a year. An explicit user strategy or horizon takes precedence. Quote-only or auxiliary updates retain the original horizon; a newly adopted forward horizon follows the model-change rules rather than silently extending an old decision.

Always browse current sources for stock research tasks. Separate sourced facts, market interpretation, and model inference. Cite links for important facts whenever possible. If the latest material filing, current issued shares, or timestamped current quote cannot be verified, withhold a rating and price bands. Treat future operating uncertainty through supported scenarios rather than requiring forecasts to be verified facts.

This skill supports research and decision support only. Do not present output as financial advice, do not imply guaranteed returns, and do not place or simulate real orders.

Route by the requested judgment: an announcement explanation, factual question, focused sentiment question or chip-only supplement answers that scope without forcing a rating, valuation model or independent-agent deep dive. An explicit buy/sell/hold judgment, or any rating actually given, requires the full rating and price-band contract; do not invent a lighter rating. Preserve every single-stock chip section, requested saving and standing overview obligations below. A requested full report still produces a saved limitations report when core evidence is insufficient.

Every single-stock analysis, including a full report, follow-up and short assessment, must contain a clearly labeled **筹码峰与成本分布** section. Use a compact paragraph for short answers. Follow `references/chip-analysis-framework.md` for the peak, distribution interpretation and dated fallback; a vendor cost summary alone does not complete peak coverage. If no distribution is available, retain the section with the specific gap and recovery condition rather than silently omitting it. This requirement does not force a full valuation or independent-agent workflow for a short answer.

## Workflow

Apply only stages relevant to the requested scope: factual explanations and focused auxiliary answers do not become valuation/adjudication tasks through the general steps below. Concise four-view investment judgments are useful when an investment judgment is requested; they are not mandatory filler for a factual explanation. Single-stock chip coverage and the saving/overview requirements remain applicable.

1. Identify the A-share target: code, short name, exchange, industry, main business, and listing board.
   For an update or deep dive, check whether the workspace has a completed earlier report for this code. If so, follow `references/follow-up-workflow.md` and retain the exact previous report and evidence versions; a first report does not need a comparison section.
2. For a single-stock deep dive, run the local evidence collector from the current workspace and read its saved JSON snapshot. Supply `--peer CODE=REASON` for each justified comparable and `--valuation-reset-date` when a verified corporate event makes older forecasts incomparable. Then run `scripts/collect_sentiment.py --code CODE --output-dir .` and read the saved stock snapshot plus its exact market-day references. Supply `--social-review-file` for directly verified posts and `--cross-market-file` for researcher-selected international references when available. Missing files remain explicit gaps. Then browse current sources: latest price context, announcements, filings, financial reports, news, policy, industry dynamics, and relevant cross-market references.
   Research recent company, sector and relevant overseas messages under `references/sentiment-framework.md`; the collector's limited news candidates do not replace that search. Save the reviewed narrative evidence, prior-expectation basis, behavioral hypotheses, counterevidence and time-matched price/sector feedback in its versioned review attachment. Prepare review files from sources the researcher can verify rather than requiring the user to supply them; disclose inaccessible sources and preserve the cited snapshots' original statuses.
   For every single-stock analysis, also run `scripts/collect_chips.py --code CODE --output-dir .` and read its exact saved version, actual data day, mode, metrics and issues. Include the available chip estimate, chart and interpretation in the required chip-peak section. When the current result is only a vendor summary or is unavailable, look for the latest usable saved local-distribution snapshot and present it separately as dated historical context under `references/chip-analysis-framework.md`; never substitute its date or peak for today's. Preserve a failed or unsupported result as a disclosed auxiliary gap, without automatically withholding a rating or changing new-money status. Short answers may use this collector independently; it does not replace core research or generate ratings.
3. Prioritize official or primary sources for financial, disclosure, regulatory, and ownership facts. Open the original CNINFO or exchange disclosure before using a material financial number, event, or publication date in a conclusion. The packet's downloaded PDF and page-linked excerpts aid review; extracted passages are candidates, not verified conclusions.
4. Analyze company quality, industry position, valuation, market behavior, catalysts, and risks. For new deep-dive/valuation workpapers, adopt verified inputs, explain operating assumptions, calculate derived/scenario results once and assess decisive sensitivity in that order under `references/research-workpaper.md` and `references/valuation-framework.md`. Distinguish current issued shares from weighted-average EPS shares, broker-index leads from verified forecasts, and margin of safety from upside. Explain what has improved, what the price already assumes, the failure case, and why participation now or waiting is justified. State the valuation fiscal year, exact horizon, end-of-horizon scenarios and their realization assumptions, decisive interim milestones, and next update. Keep fiscal-year model values distinct from supported terminal prices and from probability-weighted expected returns.
   Separately state the short-term sentiment horizon, dominant and competing narratives, evidence-backed psychological interpretation, confidence, invalidation and next observation. Distinguish attention from bullishness and short-term price pressure from fundamental value; sentiment alone does not change a rating or price ceiling.
   In follow-up research, compare new dated disclosures, share basis, financial inputs, quote and risks with the old assumptions and invalidation triggers. Recalculate an old price band when a trigger has fired; do not carry it forward from its historical report.
5. For a single-stock deep dive or explicit independent-agent request, maintain the versioned `run-record.md` under `references/multi-agent-debate.md`, including actual stages/roles, claim responses, supplement delivery, missing work and the next action. Resume readable valid work from that record. Run the independent-agent workflow below after the lead researcher has verified the shared evidence. Reference the exact workpaper version and proposed result IDs in the pending plan; agent proposals remain unadopted until the lead adjudicates them. For an ordinary short answer, use concise Bull Case, Bear Case, Risk Manager, and Portfolio View judgments in the lead researcher's own analysis.
6. When the request calls for an investment judgment or the answer actually gives a rating, apply the five-tier definitions in `references/position-building-and-risk.md` (`买入 / 增持 / 持有 / 减持 / 回避`). Separately state qualitative research confidence, its decisive reason, and the current new-money status. If that judgment is unsupported, use `数据不足，暂不评级` and distinguish a fact gap, unfinished modeling, or inputs that cannot be credibly bounded. Ordinary forecast disagreement does not by itself require withholding a rating. Any rated output, including a short answer, follows the full price-band contract below; fact/sentiment/chip-only answers need no rating label or action bands.
7. After adjudication, record the adopted rating and conditions in the new deep-dive/valuation workpaper's `final_decision`, then reuse them in the report's core conclusion and staged plan: observation, trial position, confirmation, add/reduce conditions, and re-evaluation triggers. The report header links the exact workpaper version and decision ID; its core conclusion is the authoritative reader-facing expression of the same decision. All later sections must match. Short answers retain the save-on-request rule. Evidence-first entry outside a band requires a recalculated, published band and price ceiling before any action. Without the user's allocation and risk constraints, give conditions without preset percentages. If evidence does not support action, state what would permit reassessment.
8. For a deep dive or a requested full report, complete the report and save it in the current workspace's `research/` directory. For a short answer, save only when the user asks. If evidence is insufficient, save a limitations report with no forced rating.
   At publication, use `references/research-review.md` to freeze the decisive expectations and failure path with exact report/workpaper versions, original cutoff and agreed windows. Link the saved `thesis-review[-vN].md` attachment and index it in an existing workspace review ledger. Preserve missing expectations in a limitations report rather than inventing forecasts; later reviews save new attachment versions without rewriting the original report or refreshing its decision.
9. Before calling a saved report complete, manually reconcile adopted workpaper inputs/results and `final_decision` with the core conclusion and later plan, including quote, rating, confidence, new-money status, exact boundaries/equality, necessary conditions and invalidation. Apply the workpaper's separate rules for changed fundamentals, quote-only updates and chip-only supplements. Check exact evidence versions, links, metadata and rating contract. Report agent workflow, material evidence gaps and artifact acceptance separately. Where workspace rules permit automated validation, use `scripts/validate_report.py --report PATH --workspace .` or save with `create_report.py --validate`; these check structure and references, not financial truth or action consistency. Known errors keep the artifact a draft. Sentiment gaps require disclosure. If workspace rules prohibit verification commands, leave acceptance `待用户验收` and give the user the command; do not run it or change tests.
   Reconcile the narrative attachment with the report: source availability precedes the claimed reaction, repeated coverage is deduplicated, prior expectations have evidence or an explicit gap, and psychological interpretations retain counterevidence. Separate sentiment confidence from fundamental research confidence; missing sentiment evidence does not itself fail the core rating gates.
   Manually check that the required chip-peak section contains either a sourced distribution and interpretation or an explicit peak-data gap, including any historical fallback's exact snapshot and actual date. A cost-summary table without this check leaves chip coverage incomplete. Disclose chip mode, actual data day and limitations separately from research confidence. Chip costs/peaks are estimates, not fair values, authenticated main-player holdings or a replacement for the current timestamped quote and valuation-based bands.
10. If the workspace contains `research/股票研究总览.md`, synchronize it after every new-stock or follow-up analysis, before the final response, using the Workspace Research Overview rules below. This is part of delivery, not a separate optional task.

## Independent-Agent Debate And Risk Review

Include the exact narrative-review attachment, its information cutoff, short-term horizon, prior-expectation evidence, competing psychological hypotheses and price/sector feedback in the shared brief under `references/multi-agent-debate.md`.

Keep the run record as a compact index of exact input/proposal/result versions and actual runtime identities; dispatch, delivery and returned updated views are separate events. Cite decisive facts and saved attachments without copying whole PDFs. After a quote continuation or chip-only supplement, identify inherited cutoff/review scope and current-run status separately; historical approval does not cover new material automatically. Resume only missing or affected work within the original deadlines and budget. The record adds no roles, rounds or agreement requirement.

When runtime collaboration tools are available, the lead researcher prepares one read-only brief from the saved evidence packet, the exact sentiment and chip snapshot versions (including explicit chip failure/coverage limits), and verified original sources. Include the latest complete market day, five-day gaps, social-review status, and only cross-market references with a stated transmission path and valid availability time. Give the same frozen brief to all agents. Use the runtime's `spawn_agent` tool to create separate bull and bear agents; pass each the same brief, its bounded role, the output contract in `references/multi-agent-debate.md`, and a request to avoid writing shared files. Use isolated context when supported. Wait for both first-round claims, then use `followup_task` or the equivalent tool to send each side the other's claims for one sourced rebuttal round. The lead researcher checks new source claims, records unresolved conflicts, and drafts a conditional plan. A newly verified material fact must be sent as a common supplement to both investment agents and, if already started, all risk agents for affected views to be updated before the run is called complete.

Next, create separate aggressive, conservative, and neutral risk agents to review that same plan and unresolved claims. Run roles in batches if concurrency is limited, and wait for their results before adjudicating. The lead researcher classifies objections and assesses the sensitivity of material, supported assumption disputes under `references/multi-agent-debate.md`, revises the plan at most once, and checks substantiated hard constraints. Withdraw an affected action if such a constraint remains unmet; name its evidence, material impact, and observable release condition. Investment agents need not agree on forecasts. The lead researcher makes the final Portfolio View, rating, price-band, and report decisions. Do not decide by agent vote or let an agent's conclusion override the Rating And Price-Band Contract.

Record which independent agents and rounds actually ran. Mark the run complete only when both investment agents answered every retained opposing claim and all three risk agents returned their reviews, including any update on later material facts. If a tool is unavailable or an agent fails, times out, omits a response, or returns unsupported claims, disclose the missing work and use only verified evidence. Never invent a missing agent's speech or label one researcher's role simulation as an independent-agent debate. If collaboration tools are unavailable and the user did not explicitly require independent agents, use the existing four-view judgment and label the independent-agent run `未执行`. If the user explicitly required independent agents, state `独立 Agent 辩论未执行`; for a deep dive, save a limitations report with verified evidence and incomplete status for a later supported run.

## Rating And Price-Band Contract

For every rating, state the intended holding period with exact start/end dates, valuation fiscal year, a current unadjusted quote in CNY/share with its provider trading timestamp, and source links. Use a verified original filing for material financial inputs and share basis; identify forward figures as assumptions or verified broker forecasts. Show low/base/high scenarios, distinguish fiscal-year model values from supported end-of-horizon prices and quote-relative price changes, and explain the interim evidence needed for realization. Show each action boundary formula, margin of safety, activation gates and invalidation events. If a decisive terminal assumption cannot be credibly bounded, disclose the specific modeling gap rather than relabel model-value upside as a one-year return. Use `references/valuation-framework.md` and `references/position-building-and-risk.md` for the single calculation and final-plan contracts.

- `买入 / 增持 / 持有`: give numeric CNY/share **buy** and **hold** ranges, plus an evidence-based reduce or reassess price or event. `买入 / 增持` require the current quote to be inside the effective buy range, with necessary research-level entry conditions satisfied; they cannot accompany an observation-only new-money plan. A `持有` rating can put its buy range below the current price; explain the condition for new money.
- `减持 / 回避`: say `暂不买入`; do not invent a buy range. Give only a justified reduce price or a concrete reassessment price/event. If no reliable numeric level exists, state the event condition without fabricating a number.
- If the latest material filing, share basis, or timestamped quote cannot be verified, state `数据不足，暂不评级`; do not output numeric trading ranges.

The collector does not generate ratings or price bands. Derive them in the research report after source verification; never treat a vendor PE or a model scenario as an official company forecast.

## Workspace Research Overview

When the workspace contains `research/股票研究总览.md`, maintain that file on every new-stock or follow-up analysis without asking again. Read its format and maintenance rules first. Save and read back the source report before updating the overview; for an otherwise unsaved short analysis, this standing tracking request authorizes saving a dated short note, without expanding it into a full deep dive.

Upsert only the matching six-digit ticker's row from the same adopted decision as the report's authoritative core conclusion. For new workpapers, use this mapping within the overview's existing columns; legacy reports retain their actual final section without backfilling a workpaper:

| Overview content | Adopted source and mapping |
|---|---|
| Code / name / exact report link | `target.code / name` (code stays a six-digit string); link the saved, read-back report's exact filename relative to `research/`, including its actual `-vN` |
| Rating / unrated reason | `final_decision.rating / unrated_reason`; retain the specific reason type, not an older rating |
| Confidence / decisive blocker | `confidence / confidence_reason`; preserve material `hard_constraints` evidence/impact/release conditions, keeping ordinary `watch_items` separate |
| Buy / observation range | `buy_range` endpoints from adopted `derived` IDs, exact equality, unit and `price_ceiling_ref`; preserve gates and observation-only status rather than turning a conditional range into an active buy instruction |
| Hold range | `hold_range` from the same result IDs, units and boundary equality |
| Reduce / reassess | `reassess / invalidation_events / next_update`, retaining price comparison operators, event conditions and mandatory review dates |
| New-money status / conditions | `new_money_status / necessary_conditions` and their actual satisfaction status; `user_constraints` stays separate and unknown personal budgets receive no default allocation |
| Research date / horizon / cutoff | Saved report header plus `basis.holding_horizon / valuation_fiscal_year / research_as_of`; preserve the exact horizon start/end and source, separately from the fiscal year. A local supplement retains the inherited rating date, fundamental cutoff and original horizon alongside its own update date; a default change alone does not redate historical rows |
| Reference quote / provider time | `basis.reference_quote_ref = final_decision.reference_quote_ref` points to the same input's price, CNY/share, unadjusted basis, provider trading timestamp and phase; synchronization time is not a new quote |

For `减持 / 回避`, retain `暂不买入`, with no buy/hold bands or entry ceiling. If the latest analysis cannot support a rating, replace the old row with `数据不足，暂不评级`, omit numeric trading bands and preserve its specific reason category; never substitute model scenarios or sensitivities. Unmet or unknown necessary conditions retain `仅观察 / 条件满足后复核`; reaching a price alone does not activate entry. Label drafts or unresolved conflicts `草稿 / 待核对` explicitly rather than publishing them as actionable plans; resolve conflicting final conditions before an actionable synchronization. Exclusion records, research rating and new-money status are separate: a new rating or rebound does not remove an exclusion or rewrite its basis/review conditions/user decision.

Use research date and numeric same-day version (unversioned=1; v10 follows v9), not modification time, to identify the latest report. Preserve all historical reports, their links and unrelated rows or user notes, including each other ticker's research date, quote and conditions. Keep links relative to the overview's `research/` directory. Refresh counts from actual records, excluding the overview itself and evidence attachments, rating distribution, ordering and synchronization date under the overview's existing rules; do not imply that other tickers were re-researched. Manually reconcile the updated row with the saved report and adopted workpaper, including equality, observation state, original quote time and exact version; if a superseded band remains or any condition conflicts, resolve it or mark the row pending reconciliation without active bands. Disclose any synchronization failure in the final response. This is manual single-row maintenance, not an automatic writer or a pool-wide reassessment.

An archive-only overview update copies dated historical conclusions and quote timestamps; it does not issue a fresh rating or silently refresh prices. Link each row to its exact source report for valuation formulas, evidence and complete conditions. New research still follows the full current-source and rating contracts above.
